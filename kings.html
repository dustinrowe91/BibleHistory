Reign dates follow Edwin Thiele's chronology, used in most conservative study Bibles, which counts co-reigns (a son ruling alongside his father) and so explains how the Bible's reign lengths fit together. The verdicts are the Bible's own, from the formula "he did what was right" or "he did evil in the sight of the LORD." Prophets' dates are the approximate span of their ministry.
