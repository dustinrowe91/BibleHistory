About these studies

Each study follows the same twelve steps:

  1. Original language
  2. Usage elsewhere
  3. Immediate context
  4. Cross-references by tier
  5. Theological voices
  6. Translation comparison
  7. Gospel connection
  8. Practical application
  9. Common misapplications
  10. Key contrasts
  11. Unique features
  12. Summary

Cross-references are ranked in three tiers: Tier 1 where Scripture directly quotes, fulfills or explains the passage, Tier 2 for strong thematic development, and Tier 3 for echoes that illuminate but do not settle anything. Teachers' views are summarized in our own words, not quoted. Translation comparisons give the King James text in full and a few words from other versions.

Studies marked draft were prepared with AI assistance and have not yet been checked line by line. Verify anything you plan to teach, and use the "report a problem" link on any study to flag an error.
