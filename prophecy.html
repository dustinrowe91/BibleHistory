Promise and fulfillment

Prophecy Fulfilled in Christ

Old Testament promises side by side with their fulfillment in Jesus, in the order of His life: His birth, ministry, death, resurrection, and the coming of the Spirit. "Everything written about me in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms must be fulfilled" (Luke 24:44).

Reading prophecy

Quoted and echoed
Most pairs here are made by the New Testament itself, which quotes the Old Testament words, often with "this took place to fulfill what the Lord had spoken by the prophet." These are marked Quoted in the NT. A few are echoed: the New Testament points to an event or pattern, like the Passover lamb or Jonah's three days, without quoting a prediction.
Words and patterns
Some prophecies are direct predictions, like Bethlehem in Micah 5:2. Others are patterns in Israel's story that Christ completes: Hosea 11:1 first speaks of Israel coming out of Egypt, and Matthew sees God's true Son retrace that path. Jesus taught His disciples to read all the Scriptures this way. Luke 24:25–27, 44–47
Spoken long before
The dates show how far apart promise and fulfillment stand, often seven centuries or more. Dates for the Old Testament follow the same conservative chronology as the rest of this site, and are approximate. 1 Peter 1:10–12
Quotations follow the ESV, sometimes shortened. "About N years before" counts from when the promise was given (or, for Genesis, recorded) to the event that fulfilled it. Each pair links to the Timeline and to both books on the Books page.
