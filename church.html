The measure used on this page

"Contend for the faith that was once for all delivered to the saints" (Jude 3). The church has long summed up that faith in the ecumenical creeds. This page measures every group by them:

  1. One God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, each fully God (the Nicene Creed). Matthew 28:19
  2. Jesus Christ, one person, truly God and truly man, born of the virgin, crucified, risen bodily, and coming again (the Chalcedonian Definition). John 1:1, 14
  3. Salvation by God's grace, received through faith in Christ, not earned by works. Ephesians 2:8–9
  4. Scripture as God's word, complete in the apostles, with no new revelation set beside it. 2 Timothy 3:16–17

World religions outside the Christian faith are described fairly, in their own terms, with the common ground they share, and then set beside the gospel.

The denominations in the family tree confess the creeds; they differ over important but secondary matters such as baptism, church government, the Lord's Supper, and spiritual gifts. The groups under Outside orthodoxy depart from one or more of these four points. Each is described fairly, in its own terms, before it is measured.
